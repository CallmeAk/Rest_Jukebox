# 🎵 BeatBites – Smart Restaurant Guest Engagement & Music Platform

BeatBites is a modern restaurant engagement platform that combines guest registration, music requests, customer analytics, loyalty programs, and DJ queue management into a single experience.

Guests scan a QR code at their table, register, request songs, vote on music, earn loyalty rewards, and receive notifications while restaurants gain valuable customer insights and engagement data.
