              Privacy notice

              This restaurant collects your name and mobile number (and optionally email, date of birth and favourite genre) for one purpose: to run the in-venue jukebox and loyalty programme.

              • What we use it for: taking song requests, awarding loyalty points, and — only if you opt in — sending you offers.
